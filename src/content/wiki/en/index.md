Kithin is a place to be with people, discover things and take them with you. It opens in the browser, with or without a headset.

## Where to start

- [Quick start](/wiki/getting-started/quick-start) — enter your first world.
- [Import an avatar from a model](/wiki/avatars/import-an-avatar) — turn a rigged `.glb` into your own avatar.
- [Enable HTML-in-Canvas for the Inspector](/wiki/inspector/enable-html-in-canvas) — turn on the browser flag the in-game Inspector needs.
- [Writing wiki pages](/wiki/contributing/writing-pages) — help improve this wiki.
