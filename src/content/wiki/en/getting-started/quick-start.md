## Enter a world

1. Open `/play` in your browser. You don't need an account: the lobby loads locally.
2. Press `Tab` on desktop, or the menu button on your controller, to open the **Dash** panel. From there you can sign in, browse worlds and open your inventory.
3. Pick a world, or press **Enter VR** if you have a compatible headset.
